Project Completion. The contractor shall complete all works within 18 months from the date of the Letter of Acceptance.

Liquidated Damages. If the contractor fails to complete the works on time, liquidated damages of 0.5% of the contract price per week shall apply, up to a maximum of 10% of the contract price.

Payment Terms. Running bills shall be paid within 30 days of certification by the Engineer-in-Charge.

Performance Guarantee. The contractor shall furnish a performance bank guarantee of 5% of the contract value within 15 days of the award.

Defect Liability. The defect liability period shall be 12 months from the date of completion.
